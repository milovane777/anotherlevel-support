Calculator from Mars · 시행일: 2026년 11월 3일

Calculator from Mars(이하 "앱", 개발: Another Level)는 사용자의 개인정보를 수집하지 않고, 어떤 정보도 외부 서버로 전송하지 않습니다. 앱에는 인터넷으로 통신하는 기능이 없습니다. 지구–화성 거리와 통신 지연을 포함한 모든 계산은 앱에 들어 있는 상수와 궤도 요소로 기기 안에서 이루어집니다.

1. 카메라

  • [측정] 탭의 각도 도구와 카메라 수평선 안내 도구에서, 카메라 화면 위에 각도기나 수평선을 겹쳐 맞추는 데 씁니다.
  • 촬영 버튼을 눌렀을 때만 사진을 찍습니다. 사진은 사진 보관함에 저장하지 않고, iOS 공유 창으로만 넘깁니다. 어디로 보낼지는 사용자가 고릅니다.
  • 찍은 사진은 iOS가 정리할 때까지 앱의 임시 폴더에 남아 있습니다.

2. 마이크

  • [측정] 탭의 소음 측정 도구에서 지금 주변 소리의 크기를 보여 주는 데만 씁니다.
  • 소리를 녹음 파일로 저장하지 않으며, 기기 밖으로 보내지 않습니다.

3. 위치

  • [측정] 탭의 나침반, 속도계, 고도계에서 방위, 속도, 고도를 보여 주는 데만 씁니다.
  • 나침반이 열려 있는 동안에는 iOS가 진북을 계산할 수 있도록 낮은 정확도의 위치 갱신을 요청합니다. 앱은 그 위치를 읽지 않고 버립니다.
  • 사용자가 어디에 있는지 저장하거나 기기 밖으로 보내지 않습니다.

4. 동작 센서

수평계, 다림추, 카메라 수평선 안내, 진동 도구는 기기의 가속도 센서로 기울기와 흔들림을 계산합니다. 이 값은 화면에 그리는 데만 쓰고 저장하거나 전송하지 않습니다.

광고 네트워크, 제3자 분석 도구, 추적 SDK를 포함하지 않습니다. 앱은 Flutter로 만들었고 shared_preferences(기기 안 저장), share_plus(공유 창), camera, sensors_plus, record, flutter_compass, geolocator(측정 도구), app_settings(권한 설정 화면 열기) 플러그인을 씁니다. 어느 것도 앱을 대신해 정보를 수집하거나 전송하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy

Calculator from Mars · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Calculator from Mars (“the app”), developed by Another Level (“we”), does not collect your personal data and does not send any information to a server. The app makes no network requests of any kind. Every figure, including the Earth–Mars distance and the signal delay, is computed on your device from constants and orbital elements built into the app.

Camera

The Measure tab’s Angle and Camera horizon guide tools use the camera for on-screen alignment, lining a protractor or a horizon line up against what the camera sees. A photo is taken only if you press the shutter button. It is never added to your photo library and never sent anywhere by the app itself: it goes to the iOS share sheet, and where it goes from there is your choice. Until iOS clears it, the photo stays in the app’s temporary storage on your device.

Microphone

The Sound level tool reads the microphone to show how loud the current ambient sound is. Sound is never recorded to a file, saved or sent anywhere.

Location

The Compass, Speedometer and Altimeter tools use location to show heading, speed and altitude. While the Compass is open, the app also asks iOS for low-accuracy location updates so iOS can work out true north; the app discards them without reading them. The app never stores or sends where you are.

Motion sensors

The Bubble level, Plumb level, Camera horizon guide and Vibration tools read the accelerometer to work out tilt and shaking. The reading is used only to draw the result and is never stored or sent.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (theme, decimal places, angle unit, language, number format, which trade calculators are shown, your favourites), your last 200 calculations, exchange rates you have typed in, calculators you have designed and flowcharts you have saved. This stays only on your device; we cannot access it. There is no account. If your device’s own backup (iCloud or a computer) includes app data, that copy is governed by Apple’s or your computer’s terms.

When information leaves your device (only when you choose)

  • Share buttons: the share buttons on calculation screens hand plain text to the iOS share sheet: the calculation’s title, its figures and any working or source note, then the app’s name and a short disclaimer. The Mars age card hands over one line of text, and the shutter buttons of the Angle and Camera horizon guide tools hand over a photo. You choose where it goes; the app does not know or record your choice.

Permissions

  • Camera: angle measurement and the horizon guide, and a photo when you press the shutter (not saved or sent by the app).
  • Microphone: the sound level reading (nothing recorded or sent).
  • Location (while using the app): compass heading, speed and altitude (not stored or sent).

iOS asks for each permission the first time the related tool is opened. If a permission is off, that tool shows a plain message and the rest of the app works as usual.

Third parties

The app includes no advertising networks, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It is built with Flutter and uses the shared_preferences (on-device storage), share_plus (share sheet), camera, sensors_plus, record, flutter_compass, geolocator (measuring tools) and app_settings (opening Settings) plugins. None of them collects or sends data on the app’s behalf.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Swipe a row in the History tab to delete that calculation, or delete the app to erase everything on the device.
